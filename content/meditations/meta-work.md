my agents tell me i work around optimizing work more than i just do the work.  

i like higher order work. the problem lies around not getting things done.

explicitly enforcing some constraints to restore balance.

 - work on week-days, just as the usual human does.  
 - meta-work on week-nights, been enjoying some caffeinated work-raves lately.
 - weekend-days and nights are deliberately planned to be unplanned - i'll let my whims takeover and do whatever i'm more attracted towards

feels kinda sad to assert that i'll not be building any thing that builds stuff better when i'm building stuff. 

also feels good to say that i'll be only building stuff that builds stuff during a certain part of the day  - every day will present a night to look forward to.
