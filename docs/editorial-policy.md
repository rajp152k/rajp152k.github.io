# Editorial policy

## Reader review

Read the complete draft. Identify its purpose and intended reader. Check names,
assumed knowledge, claims, source links, and earlier posts that help the reader.
Keep questions outside the article. Ask raj before changing meditation prose.
Create agent logs only when requested.

## Terms

Ask: would a short definition help this reader understand this passage without
leaving the article?

Include a term when the name has a clear, specific meaning and the available
sources support a useful short definition. A concept can need a definition on
its first appearance. Repeated use is evidence of usefulness, not a requirement.

For each candidate, choose one action:

1. Reuse a saved term with the same meaning.
2. Add or revise a supported term record.
3. Suggest an explanation in the article when a short definition is insufficient.
4. Exclude it because it is familiar to the intended reader, incidental, already
   explained, ambiguous, or unsupported. Record important exclusions and reasons.

Use stable IDs. Prefer full phrases for ambiguous names. Read the source context,
not only a matching word. Save exact source passages in terms.json. A mention
alone does not support a definition. Choose a useful introductory post, not the
first mention by date. Review affected older posts before changing a shared
meaning. Do not combine different meanings under one definition.

## References and related reading

Check source links and distinguish evidence from further reading. Select earlier
posts for a specific reader need and record why they help. Shared vocabulary or
embedding similarity alone is insufficient. Check the selected post itself.
Term cards show introductions, evidence, and matched mentions; an incidental
mention is not a recommended introduction. Record additional reading choices in
the references review. Ask raj before adding these links to meditation prose.

## Decisions and questions

The agent maintains supporting records and performs routine checks. Ask raj when
intent or meaning is uncertain, or a proposed change affects the argument. Do not
ask for approval of every routine term record.

Omit an uncertain optional definition and record the question. Optional questions
can accompany a draft or release. Mark a question as blocking when it prevents a
sound reader or reference review. Resolve it explicitly before recording that
stage again. Record only actual reviews of the current inputs.

A post may need no terms or related reading. Record that judgment and its reason.
Mechanical checks establish record integrity and review freshness. They do not
establish that a definition follows from its source or that a reader review is
correct. Raj approves publication separately.
