# Editorial policy

## Authority

Raj approves terms and post links. The agent proposes candidates, checks evidence,
and records raj's decisions. Apply only the approved scope and revision. Approval
of a draft, a build, or a commit is not term or link approval.

Use the [workflow](workflow.md) for states and gates and the
[curation guide](curation.md) for candidate records. These rules apply now. The
Markdown state format is a first version for review; scripts do not enforce it yet.

## Reader review

Read the complete draft. Identify its purpose, intended reader, and assumed
knowledge. Check names, claims, sources, and possible reading links. Keep questions
outside the article. Ask raj before changing meditation prose. Draft logs only
when requested.

## Term candidates

Submit jargon and domain-specific terms. A phrase made from common words does not
qualify merely because it has a local meaning in a post. Technical phrases can
qualify when there is evidence of an established domain meaning. Show that evidence.
A product name, repeated phrase, or useful summary is not enough by itself.

Then ask whether a short definition would help the intended reader understand the
passage. Explain the domain, meaning, reader need, and supporting source. Propose
reuse, addition, revision, or removal; raj decides. Include proposed uses of an
existing term in the review, not just new definitions.

Keep proposed records in the review document until approved. Submit the exact
name, definition, aliases, evidence, introduction, and affected post uses together.
Raj may approve candidates as a named batch. Silence is not approval.

Read source context, not only a matching word. Save exact archive passages for
approved records in `terms.json`. A mention alone does not support a definition.
External sources can establish domain usage during review; the current registry
stores archive passages as definition evidence. Report any missing evidence.

Use stable IDs and precise aliases. Keep different meanings separate. Show the
impact on older posts before changing a shared definition or alias. Reopen approval
when its meaning, wording, evidence, aliases, introduction, or approved uses change.

## Links and introductions

Propose each introduction, evidence link, and related-reading link with its source,
target, purpose, and exact placement or registry field. Read the target. Explain
what the reader gains. Shared words, embedding similarity, and first mention by
date do not establish a useful link. Distinguish a citation from further reading.

Raj approves additions, changes, and removals of curated post links, including
those in logs. List existing links and term matches in the post's review so raj
can confirm them or request changes. Automatic matches and map lines are derived
output, not evidence of approval. Preview them after applying approved changes.

## Decisions and questions

Record approval, rejection, deferral, and requested revisions with their scope and
raj's reason when supplied. Keep the decision history. The agent may screen out
ineligible candidates before submission; make important exclusions visible.

If no terms or links are needed, submit that result for raj's confirmation. Omit
unresolved optional additions and ask raj to defer them explicitly. An unresolved
required explanation or source blocks curation. Do not infer a decision from a
successful check or an old agent review.

Record only reviews actually performed on the current inputs. Mechanical checks
establish integrity and freshness, not editorial quality or raj's approval.
Commit and publication have separate approval gates.
