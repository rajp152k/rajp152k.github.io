---
title: Meta-work
date: 2026-08-18
---

I can spend too much time improving the system around work and too little time doing the work itself.

I am testing a simple constraint:

- weekdays are for direct work;
- weeknights are for tools, process, and other meta-work;
- weekends remain unplanned.

The rule is not a theory of productivity. It is a guardrail against replacing progress with increasingly elaborate preparation.
