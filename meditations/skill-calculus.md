i've been using pi as my daily driver for a while now - i'm maintaining my personal set of skills, extensions and usage heurestics here - https://github.com/rajp152k/pi-exts  

having taken some inspiration from the unix philosophy, i'm trying to decompose the usual skills one would use (do something higher order that encapsulates a bunch of stuff) into composible short ones (decide whether this could be a background/active job (syncs, research dispatches) , then figure out what is the right modality to dispatch this (a separate tmux window, in the main agent session, coordinated subagent ..) , how do you consolidate the findings meaningfully (persist them onto a file, inject/steer context into/of an existing session) , does the human need to be notified of what was done, does progress need to be observable..) that these higher order skills would use.  

i can anticipate the usefullness of thinking this through but am not there yet in terms of how i'll actually set it all up  

one basic idea was to have a higher order collection of meta-skills that document the heurestics around how does one compose the lower level hard skills - that also had me thinking if markdowns are the right way to persist how one would structure these lower order skills or should i maintain a sqlite db that brings along a whole bunch of niceties  

another thing i'm focusing on is trying to find minimal slices that i could work on well instead of planning the whole thing from the get go and using it in the end - instead , usage through the iterations drives feedback  

a bunch of names come to mind - skillfu, skilling in the name, skill issues fixing skill,  but skill calculus sounds just the right amount of pretentious and ambitious
