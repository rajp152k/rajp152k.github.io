I'm whetting my appetite to work in solitude again. I'd been smitten by the idea of maintain personal research lab long ago - I can't find the article that incited that thought: it was mostly along the lines of personal science.  

Quite a romantic aesthetic around a man hammering away at projects in a cave with some good acid techno. Think a (neo-engineer , arcane-wizard) archetype amalgam.  

I've been going through a cognitive trough lately (been a year or so) - where I'm not pushing myself hard enough towards the kind of ambition I used maintain around my side projects.  

The most different sort of happiest (I don't like using that word but here we are) I've been in my life has been when I'm thinking about hard problems - haven't quite invested much into those lately and that's taken a toll on my cognitive health.  

This site as it stands right now serves as a collection of my laboratory notes. There's more I slowly plan on integrating over time but I'm going to be deliberate about it.  

I've ditched thinking through the fixed pipeline of pushing markdown through static site generators and instead am experimenting what all i could do in decoupled build stages - thinking more natively in the form higher order abstractions like data and transformations, where do i house / run each of them..  

This being my fourth or fifth personal cyber space over the span of 7+ years, have deliberately let go of all the rules. All previous moves were from the perspective of getting into a formulaic setup - this time, feels like slowly furnishing up a large empty warehouse - the initial days would be .., well .., a little empty, but I should pretty quickly converge on my own style.  

I plan on being a more mathematically inclined individual in this phase of my life - applied pragmatic mathematics is the primary higher order field that will influence the ideas that i have in the foreseeable future. So this is more of a think-out-loud in terms of what all i'd like in my personal research lab.  

I'm also a linguistics person and there was a time in one my past spaces where i used to maintain my own dictionary around words i coined to succinctly represent concepts that took more space. Thinking of bringing back a flavour of that in here.  

Computational epistemology is another domain that I'm building up cognitive senses for..  

There's a bunch more and i don't let the inter-domain boundaries wall me off from jumping those pseudo fences - i'll bring them out one by one over a bunch of future meditations..  

I do miss being ambitious for the sake of itself - going to chase that high again.
