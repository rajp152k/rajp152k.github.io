i've been lacking a creative fix lately - head hurts in a bad way - feels like the lack of a certain form of use is resulting in a decay of sorts...  

i'm not the kind to be into in to pre-thought-out hobbies and like engineering merges of my own - wondering what i get into this time - i've been missing some screen time 
 lately - contrary to what most humans like, i like sleeping on grass and glueing my eyes into a stark green terminal at the same time - am fortunate enough to really enjoy just typing away for some reason - don't really know why - going to continue enjoying that while it lasts

lately, i've also stopped thinking of how i do things - i.e. given identical outputs, elegance is a non-factor - that has been somewhat liberating in the sense of liberating more bandwidth for what i really want to get done - brutalist GTD or something like that comes to mind 

i'd been pulled in by variants of electronically generated music a while back but didn't really fool around much before i could gather critical inertia of intent  

i'd like to explore something experimentally though - instead of reading up a bunch of textbooks on it - there's this nice one on the math of music but i don't like reading textbooks anymore (lately) unless i really have to

thinking of a daemon that's capable of ingesting generic input tensors reactively producing generic output tensors - could be a time-series of whatever - holograms, electronic music or just wall art via a projector - i could spend days in such dark caves  

a while ago, i used to be quite emotionally attached and delusional about my hobbies - like what if i could be that guy playing the yamaha piano on airports or pulling off mentalism party tricks - am quite joyfully dettached right now - seems like progress  

will invest some time over the next several "raj needs a new hobby" mental cycles into this daemon - as always the best part about any side project is coming up with a good name - got a meditation fix for my next walk
