writing and curating is quite non-linear and involves a bunch of recursive refinement around ideas. My intent around these meditations is to "write to think" (realized during that journal entry) - that involves being a little more transparent and honest around what my brain is really upto when i'm thinking of a particular thing  

i don't care about punctuation or grammar or spelling well as much when i think - i understand why i do care about it so muc hwhen i publish  

writing for the sake of publishing feels off lately - why do i have to publish in a curated and polished manner - that is not really what i'm going for these days  

anyway don't like reading extremely curated stuff adn would just prefer the idea being conveyed as efficiently and rigorously as possible when i do to try that  

in the middle of this all , realized that i'm not this person to force myself to write like 1000 words a day and rack my brain around if a sentence sounds right?  

i'd rather iterate upon a 1000 essays, run higher order tooling and processing on those (LLM assisted summaries, visualize embeddings, note patterns, etc) to let the ideas emerge , instead of trying to force a conveyance in that one essay.  

bear with me while i figure out how does one write to think  

i shall get back to the idea of how do i really want to approach knowledge and meta-knowledge and my incentives around it ove rtime - that is somethign that deserves more than an essay and a lot more honesty

i'll be spending some more time evolving this process and being a whole lot emore honest aroudn how i like to analyse myt approach to maitnaing my ideas, in a compute assisted manner
