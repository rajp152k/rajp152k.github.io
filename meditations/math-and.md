When I meet a new idea, I ask whether it is mathematical, might become mathematical, or currently resists that treatment.

Mathematics is useful when it gives a small set of objects, relations, and constraints that explain more than the original description. The pleasure is compression: a vague thing becomes a model that can be inspected and changed.

Not every useful thought has that form. Some judgments remain practical, aesthetic, or experiential. The boundary is not fixed. A question can move from "not yet formalized" to "formalizable" when a better representation appears.
