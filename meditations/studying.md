Studying feels good - be that following a structured curriculum or unstructured exploration without any intent.  

The way one should be studying, given the times, is the subject of my meditations, fairly often now.  

Acquaintances lie across a spectrum:  some would power through corpora e2e, while some leverage LLMs to produce artifacts and wouldn't bat an eye over the raw docs.  

A bit of both, based on my mood in the moment, is what I do..  

Retrospecting, my studying bouts exhibit a few repeating patterns.  

Understanding all the domain specific terms independent of the text is what i do first. This might look like working in an outline - i picked this habit up from all the years spent in emacs and org mode. A tree data structure to represent concepts and recursing to the leaf nodes (constituent sub-concepts) that i comprehend is the first step i take towards breaking down a larger problem. I make sure i understand the tree from the bottom up if i have enough time (when this is a leisurely studying bout). When dealing with deadlines, compromises around being okay with just knowing the higher level "whats" around a node need to be made. This might lead to some back and forth when i hit a wall during a task, and i have to invest some more in the depth of a particular subtree.  

Next is a context-aware pass of the tree as a whole. That is, not just understanding the nodes independently but enriching what they mean given the other nodes in the tree.  

I build and maintain this concept tree (could be a markdown or an org file). I could get answers from the raw (not much really stays unparsed by an LLM these days, I believe, though) doc or through intent focused artifacts (generated with an intent in mind).  

Given this initial hierarchical outline (concept tree), given varying intent, i'll fork it off and iterate.  

This is one of the ways I like to approach a structured task.  

Unstructured exploration around a domain as a whole, when i don't have a strong seeding intent, would be treated separately in a future post.
