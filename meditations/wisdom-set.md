---
title: Wisdom set
date: 2026-08-11
---

The problem is not merely retaining more information. It is deciding what information deserves attention and how it should change future judgment.

I want to collect instances of learning: what was encountered, how it was interpreted, what action followed, and whether that interpretation survived contact with reality.

Over time, the collection may reveal recurring criteria for what is worth knowing. Until then, "wisdom set" is a name for the question, not a system.
