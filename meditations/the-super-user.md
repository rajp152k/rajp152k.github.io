title: the super user
date: 2026-08-26
---

i really like complexity, when i really want to like it. design folks will bucket me as a progressive disclosure afficionado or something.  

usually, the world dumbs stuff down for you into only the abstractions and interfaces that you need - that's good when you know what you wish to get done  

i'm more of a "i'll figure it out as to what i wish to figure out while i'm figuring it out" sorta person - transparent abstractions help accelerate your incentive vectors better when you're upto such unstructured shenanigans  

that got me thinking that no one ever explicitly builds for the super user, except when the super user builds stufffor personal convenience just makes it public  

this super user mindset bleeds kinda everywhere - i've been implicitly enjoying it for a while - asking too many questions way before i learn the basics with the intention to just understand what's truly up instead of just getting on with it  

one of my bed time projects right now is building something geared towards superusers and i'm actually enjoying spending thoughts around what makes an interface "super worthy"

the feeling i'd like to go for is making them feel like they're swerving through traffic smoothly on a motorcycle - that is what i feel what being a super user feels like   

the mouse is obviously out if i'm doing that - CLIs are good for agents but a very intuitively designed TUI that behave like the HUD of that motorcycle is where i'm at  

will borrow from a bunch of different tooling design philosophies i've enjoyed over the years  

the first thing i think pays off the most is desiging the language and grammar that the interface enables - chaining keystrokes and chords then into what you want to do seems easy

i shall meditate out loud more upon this, more often
