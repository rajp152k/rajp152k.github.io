title: Applied epistemology
date: 2026-08-14

I want a more explicit account of how I decide what to believe, study, and act on.

The starting material is not clean. It is years of reading, experience, and habits of inference that I have rarely named. The task is to inspect that material, separate useful models from inherited noise, and make the resulting judgments easier to revise.

This is a study program, not a declaration of expertise.
